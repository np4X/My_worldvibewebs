About

Prompt-first, always.

I build things by having a conversation, not by grinding through documentation. A little prompt, a follow-up, a nudge in the right direction — and a working site comes out the other end.

Underneath the tool, it's a plain skill: turning a vague ask into a precise one, then iterating with a collaborator until it's right. That's communication and problem-solving — the same skill client-facing engineering work runs on, just aimed at a different collaborator.

No framework loyalty, no "real developer" gatekeeping. Just me, an AI, and enough curiosity to keep iterating until it feels right.

I mostly vibe in the languages I already have some footing in — LaTeX, Python, a little Arduino. Enough to know what's going on, AI fills in the rest.

My rule: ship the vibe first, figure out what broke after. Every bug is just the next thing I learn — I'm not pretending to have it all figured out, I'm figuring it out in public.

Off-screen, I'm an Electrical Engineering student at Chulalongkorn University — that side of my work is hands-on, hand-built, no shortcuts. Robots, circuits, control systems. Different tool, same curiosity.
